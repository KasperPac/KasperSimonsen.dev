# KS agency preview

User brief, 12 November 2026: create an approachable agency site for business owners. Clearly explain services, business types and sectors, small-business support, and how to make contact. The business name is undecided. Industrial automation is linked separately, not a core agency service. Use the existing Figma board as visual inspiration.

The first draft lives at `/agency`, in a separate worktree based on production commit `34acdd8`. Nothing is published. Claude's uncommitted `oneshot-office` worktree remains intact.

Design decisions for this draft: use KS as a provisional name, with “Digital services” as a descriptor. Use the inspected Figma social's paper `#f0eee7`, ink `#172b36`, slate `#486b7d` and mist `#b9cbd3`, IBM Plex Sans and restrained pen marks. This is a site direction, not a new practice design system.

Content order: clear offer and contact action; four services; AI agents and business optimisation; dedicated small-business support; business types and example needs; invitation to the interactive office; working process and founder; contact. Visitors can read everything by scrolling or use ordinary section links. Additional service detail uses native disclosures. No 3D, scroll interception, hidden hotspots or performance claims on the agency page.

Services: websites, business software, AI and automation, ongoing digital support. Sectors describe potential fit rather than claiming completed projects in every industry. All contact actions use the existing email address or existing contact API. No new provider, advertised service level, price, team size, testimonial or performance statistic is invented.

Follow-up on 12 November: Kasper requested explicit AI/agent and business-optimisation positioning, and supplied the Claude architect certification claim. The page now covers custom agents, knowledge assistants and AI strategy/optimisation, with an illustrative enquiry-to-review workflow. “Claude Certified Architect” is attributed to Kasper, using the generic credential name. No Foundations/Professional level, credential ID, personal badge link or company partner status was supplied or invented. The “About the certification” link goes to Anthropic's programme page (https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications), not a personal credential verification.

The name, final service scope, geographical coverage and public copy remain reviewable draft choices. The agency is described as led by Kasper; it does not imply a staffed team. Production-domain decisions and any migration of Claude's personal portfolio are separate from this local preview.

Portfolio follow-up on 12 November: Kasper requested removing the project gallery and linking creatively to the interactive office. The gallery and conventional portfolio links are replaced by “The door’s open” at `/agency#the-office`, with a simple sketched doorway using the approved KS mark. Its door opens further on hover or keyboard focus; reduced-motion mode keeps it still. The whole invitation is one ordinary link to `/office`, without prefetching or loading office models on the agency page. The navigation and footer now point to this invitation or the office itself.

Design round on 12 November (built by Claude at Kasper's request, after he chose from lettered mock-ups): Kasper picked four things. (1) The hero shows real client work, Marianne's Hair in a browser frame and on a phone, in place of the drawn mock browser. (2) Services sit two by two, each with its sketch from the Figma board on a mist plate. (3) The AI section is an ink band, with the example as three numbered steps; its second paragraph and the “One example” footnote were cut. (4) One accent, a warm yellow `#f4bf2a`, used only as a fill: the main contact button of a section, the form's send button, the tick badges and the ticked part of each sketch. The header button stays ink. Handwriting is down to two notes (the hero and the door) and the tilted cards are straightened. He also keeps “we” as the voice; the agency will launch under its own name, still undecided, and kaspersimonsen.dev stays his portfolio. At his instruction the hero caption calls Marianne's Hair a “salon website and custom booking platform”, not only a website: the booking platform is the owner's diary built for the salon, with booking records kept in Square. He then asked for the Manuva home page in the hero as the more colourful piece, in both frames. He asked for the last scene of its scroll animation (feet up, the logo on the wall, the lime highlight), so both frames show captures of the live site taken that day: `public/agency/manuva-end-desktop.jpg` and `public/agency/manuva-end-phone.jpg`. The highlighted word in that headline rotates on the live site; both captures were taken on “making”. The caption reads “Manuva / Manufacturing software and its website”. Marianne's Hair is no longer on the page; if it returns, the booking-platform wording above applies.

Later the same day he asked for the AI section to have something other than text and chose “example screens”: each of the three steps shows the screen a person would see (the customer's message, the agent's checklist, a draft reply waiting for approval). The example, a painter answering a quote request, is made up and labelled “One example” on the page; its wording is a draft in `aiExample` in `agency/content.ts`, drawn by `agency/AgentExample.tsx`. The screens are pictures, hidden from assistive technology, where each step's description stands in.

Section headers, 13 November: Kasper said the section headers were too easy to miss. From four mock-ups (louder labels, bigger headlines, both, yellow tags) he chose the yellow tags. Each section's name now sits on a strip of the accent yellow, in ink: 17px medium, 15px on phones, on light and dark bands alike. Headline sizes are unchanged. This gives the yellow a second job beside the main buttons and the ticks. The small line in the hero describes the business, is not a section name, and stays slate.

Release, 13 November: Kasper asked for the page to go live at kaspersimonsen.dev/agency. It keeps `noindex, nofollow` from its layout, it is not in the sitemap, and nothing on the portfolio links to it, so it is reachable by its address only. The enquiry form uses the portfolio's existing contact API.

Where things are: the hero project is `heroWork` in `agency/content.ts`; `agency/HeroWork.tsx` draws the frames. Section names are `.section-label` in `app/(agency)/agency.css`. The sketches are generated artwork in `agency/sketches.ts` (Figma node `196:427`), drawn by `agency/ServiceSketch.tsx`, which replaces `ServiceIcon.tsx`. Ticks are `agency/Tick.tsx`. The accent is the `--accent` token at the top of `app/(agency)/agency.css`. Open points: the Figma board's own desktop device frame shows the salon's phone number, which must stay blurred, so do not use that frame; the posts and the Figma variables do not carry the yellow yet.
